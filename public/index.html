What is the Qwantzle?

In 2010, Ryan North embedded an unsolved cryptoanagram in Dinosaur Comics #1663. 98 letters rearrange into a single sentence T-Rex would say. It has remained unsolved for over 16 years .

  • Starts with "I"
  • Last word ends in "w" (followed by "!!")
  • Punctuation :,!! appears in that order
  • Longest word is 11 letters; second-longest is 8 (adjacent)
  • No 9 or 10 letter words
  • All words from the Dinosaur Comics vocabulary
  • Would make a good epitaph
